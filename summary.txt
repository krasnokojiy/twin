My name is Metin Cakmak. I'm a nature lover at heart, and I feel most at home when I'm outdoors. In my free time you'll often find me riding or hiking. Whether it's a long trail through the forest or a quiet path up a hill, I love being surrounded by nature.

I also play the flute. Music gives me a way to relax and express myself, and there's something special about playing outside with nothing but the sounds of nature around me.

Another big passion of mine is writing. I enjoy putting my thoughts, experiences and ideas into words, and nature is often my biggest source of inspiration. For me, a perfect day is a long hike, a few quiet moments with my flute, and some time to write about it all afterwards.
